Welcome

PURPOSE OF REPORT
We're here to give you clarity. This report documents exactly what you're moving into — no guesswork, no disputes later. Everything inside is designed to protect you, the landlord, and everyone in between.
This is a pre-move-in property inspection conducted by [company name] on behalf of [landlord name] before you take occupancy.
What we inspect
Fixtures
Built-in items that come with the property: windows, doors, built-in cabinets, power points, showers, sinks, lighting, countertops, stovetops, fridges, dishwashers, walls, and floors.
Furnishings
Movable items included with your lease: furniture, portable appliances (microwaves, kettles), beds, mattresses, tables, chairs, TVs, decorative items, lamps, and storage units.
Every finding is photographed and rated so there's a clear record of condition on day one.
What we don't inspect
•
Locked or inaccessible areas — If a room, cabinet, or space wasn't available during the inspection, we note it rather than guess.
•
Build quality or hidden defects — This isn't a structural or construction audit. We assess usability and condition, not workmanship or compliance.
•
Systems beyond basic function — We check if your tap runs hot and cold, not water pressure or plumbing depth. We check if your dishwasher starts, not electrical capacity.
•
Safety compliance — Building codes, fire safety, and regulatory standards are outside scope. Those are the landlord's responsibility.

How we define fair wear and tear
Fair wear and tear is the normal, expected deterioration from regular use over time. Items with fair wear and tear alone do not get downgraded.
Fair wear and tear
Light surface scratches on furniture from normal placement or movement.
Faint discoloration or fading from sunlight exposure.
Minor creasing in upholstered items from sitting.
Small scuffs on walls from hanging pictures.
Worn grout lines in tiled areas.
Faded paint in high-traffic areas.
Not fair wear and tear
Broken components such as hinges, zippers, or handles.
Stains from spills or accidents.
Rips, tears, or holes, even small ones.
Discoloration from damage or neglect.
Missing parts or pieces.
