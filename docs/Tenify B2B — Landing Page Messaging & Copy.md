# Tenify B2B — Landing Page Messaging & Copy

*Date: 2026-08-14 | Owner: Lord Tak*

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** Tenify B2B turns your pre-move-in inspection into a branded, revenue-generating service — done in minutes, not manually.

**Who it's for:** Letting agencies and property management companies in Dubai who already run pre-move-in inspections for the landlords they manage, but do it manually — paper notes, camera roll photos, inconsistent write-ups, no defensible record.

**What changes:** Their inspectors use the same guided, AI-assisted app Tenify's own certified agents use — but every report carries the agency's own logo, generates in minutes, and can be sold as a paid add-on to landlords and tenants.

This is distinct from the consumer/DTC Tenify product (tenant books an independent inspection). B2B is a tool the agency owns and operates under its own brand — see [[B2B_MODEL_STRATEGY]] and `Tenify B2B PRD.md` for the underlying product.

---

## 2. Messaging Pillars

Five pillars, in priority order for an agency-first (business) buyer:

### Pillar 1 — Differentiate
No competitor offers a detailed pre-move-in report today. This is a real point of difference to win landlord and tenant business, not a commodity feature everyone already has.

### Pillar 2 — New revenue
Sell it as a paid add-on to landlords, to tenants, or both. Per-seat pricing keeps the agency's cost predictable regardless of inspection volume.

### Pillar 3 — Your brand, not ours
Every report carries the agency's logo, not Tenify's. It reinforces the agency's brand at the single moment landlords and tenants are paying closest attention — move-in.

### Pillar 4 — Protection for everyone
- **Tenants** get an exact record of move-in condition, protecting their deposit from disputes at move-out.
- **Landlords** get a complete picture of every part of the property, not just a list of faults.
- **Agencies** get an auditable, defensible record instead of notes and photos scattered across WhatsApp.

### Pillar 5 — Faster, more consistent
PDF report generated within minutes of finishing the walkthrough. Every inspector follows the same guided flow, so quality doesn't depend on who did the walkthrough.

## 3. Proof Points / Supporting Facts

Use these as the specific, credible backup for each pillar claim — avoid vague "revolutionary" language, ground every claim in a mechanism:

| Claim | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| "No booking needed" | Ad-hoc report creation — inspector enters property/tenant details on the spot, no pre-existing Tenify booking required |
| "Consistent inspections" | Same guided room → item → assessment flow every time, pre-populated rooms, AI-assisted rating with human override |
| "Report in minutes" | Single batched AI call per room, PDF generated automatically on completion |
| "Your brand" | Company logo uploaded once, applied automatically to every report's header |
| "Defensible record" | Structured Must Fix / Recommended Fixes summary + itemised, photo-evidenced detail per room |
| "Per-seat pricing" | Tiered seat-band billing, one company account, admin manages users and billing centrally |

---

## 4. Objections to Pre-empt

- *"We already send someone to check the property."* → The gap isn't whether an inspection happens, it's whether it's structured, photographed, and defensible. Manual notes and camera-roll photos don't hold up in a dispute.
- *"Will this slow my inspectors down?"* → Guided flow is built to be faster than freeform notes — pre-populated rooms, tap-to-rate, no separate report-writing step afterward.
- *"Do I need Tenify's brand on my reports?"* → No. Reports are 100% co-branded to the agency; Tenify is invisible to the landlord/tenant.
- *"Is this expensive to roll out across my team?"* → Per-seat pricing, one company account, self-serve web signup — no integration project required.
