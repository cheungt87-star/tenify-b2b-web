# B2B Model Strategy — Tenify

**Owner:** Lord Tak  
**Date:** 2026-07-04  
**Status:** Strategic Framework

---

## Executive Summary

Tenify will pursue a **dual-channel go-to-market**: direct-to-consumer (DTC) combined with B2B partnerships with estate agents and brokers. The B2B model addresses two distinct markets with tailored value propositions:

1. **Estate agents/brokers** gain a **differentiation tool** and **revenue stream**
2. **Landlords and tenants** receive pre-move-in inspection reports as a bundled service benefit

---

## 1. B2B Value Proposition

### For Estate Agents / Brokers

**Current State:**
- Minimal differentiation between agents — primarily compete on property inventory access
- No value-add services beyond matching landlord/tenant/property

**With Tenify B2B:**
- **Competitive differentiator:** "Our agents include professional pre-move-in inspections"
- **Revenue uplift:** Earn commissions or markup on inspection fees
- **Service bundling:** Transform from transactional (property match) to advisory (risk mitigation)
- **Credibility boost:** Inspection report co-branded, positioned as part of tenancy agreement addendum
- **Reduced liability:** Documented property condition protects all parties (agent, landlord, tenant)

---

### For Landlords

**Value Proposition:**

> *"Let us lease your property and receive a detailed, impartial inspection report documenting the exact condition when the tenant moves in. Protect your property and simplify deposit disputes."*

**Benefits:**
- Establishes baseline property condition at lease start
- Reduces frivolous or unjustified deposit claims
- Third-party credibility strengthens position in disputes
- Inspection report can be contractually linked (tenancy addendum)
- Faster lease-signing process with agent handling coordination

---

### For Tenants

**Value Proposition:**

> *"Know the exact condition of your property before you move in. Our detailed inspection safeguards your deposit from pre-existing damage and gives you proof if disputes arise."*

**Benefits:**
- Pre-move-in baseline protects against false damage claims
- Professional documentation backed by Tenify's credentials
- Reduces financial risk of deposit forfeiture
- Peace of mind and transparency in the leasing process
- Can be bundled at no additional cost (if agent absorbs or subsidises)

## 2. B2B Go-to-Market Models

### Model A: Referral (Commission-Based)

**How It Works:**
- Estate agent refers customer (landlord or tenant) to Tenify
- Customer pays Tenify DTC price (e.g., AED 300–600 per inspection)
- Tenify pays agent a commission on completed inspection (e.g., 15–25% of booking value)
- No volume commitment; low friction entry for agents

**Revenue Impact:**
- **Tenify cost:** Lower CAC than DTC acquisition; higher than internal referral but organic
- **Agent benefit:** Passive revenue; low operational burden
- **Customer experience:** Seamless referral; standard Tenify booking flow

**Example Economics:**
- DTC booking: AED 500
- Commission to agent: AED 75–125
- Tenify net: AED 375–425
- **Comparison:** DTC only keeps full AED 500, but DTC CAC typically 20–30% of value

**Use Case:**
- Entry-level partnerships with small, independent agents
- Low-risk pilots to test appetite
- Agents who want revenue without operational integration

---

### Model B: Wholesale (Volume + Co-Branding)

**How It Works:**
- Estate agency commits to annual or quarterly volume targets (e.g., 50+ inspections/quarter)
- Receives **preferred B2B pricing** (e.g., AED 200–300 per report, 40–50% discount to DTC)
- Tenify provides co-branded reports with agency branding, logo, and positioning
- Agency absorbs cost; bundles into service offering (pass-through to customer or margin)
- Tenify pays **volume kickback** once thresholds are met (e.g., 5% of net revenue at 100+ inspections/quarter)

**Revenue Impact:**
- **Tenify:** Predictable volume, reduced CAC, potential strategic partnership
- **Agent:** Lower per-report cost; ability to bundle without passing full cost to customer; volume incentives
- **Customers:** Bundled inspection (possibly subsidised by agent)

**Example Economics:**
- DTC price: AED 500
- B2B wholesale price: AED 250
- Agency margin (if passed to customer at AED 450): AED 200 gross
- Tenify kickback at 100+ inspections/quarter: 5% of (AED 250 × 100) = AED 1,250 bonus
- **Volume benefit:** Reduces CAC, increases predictability; agents invest in marketing the bundled service

**Use Case:**
- Larger estate agencies with 5+ agents
- Brokerages managing 50+ active landlord or tenant relationships
- Partners willing to position inspections as a core differentiator

---

### Model C: Hybrid (Referral + Upgrade Path)

**How It Works:**
- Start with referral model (low friction, no commitment)
- Agents who generate 20+ referrals/quarter are eligible to "upgrade" to wholesale
- Provides flexibility and organic escalation

**Use Case:**
- Scaling partnerships; learn agent appetite before committing
- Reduces perceived risk for agents testing the model

## 3. Co-Branding & Contract Integration

### Co-Branded Reports

**Current (DTC):**
- Tenify logo, branding, and footer

**B2B Wholesale:**
- Agency logo, branding added to header/footer
- Report framed as: *"[Agency Name] Property Condition Report, Powered by Tenify"*
- Positions inspection as agency value-add, not outsourced vendor service

**Legal & Contractual Integration:**
- Report can be referenced in tenancy agreement as **addendum or exhibit**
- Language: *"The attached Property Condition Report dated [date], conducted by [Agency Name] via Tenify, documents the property condition at lease commencement"*
- Strengthens evidentiary value and institutional credibility

## 4. Pricing & Commission Structure

### DTC Pricing (Reference)

Assumed current DTC pricing by property type:
- Studio / 1-bed flat: AED 300–350
- 2-bed flat: AED 400–450
- 3-bed villa: AED 500–600
- 4+ bed villa: AED 600–800

*(Pricing subject to your current rate card)*

---

### B2B Referral Commission

**Commission Structure:**

| Volume Tier | Commission | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 inspections/month | 15% | Entry tier; higher percentage to incentivise adoption |
| 11–25 inspections/month | 18% | Volume discount to Tenify; improved profitability |
| 26+ inspections/month | 20–25% | High-volume partner; top-tier rate |

**Example:**
- Agency refers 50 inspections/month at avg. AED 400 DTC price
- Commission: 20% × AED 400 × 50 = AED 4,000/month (AED 48k/year)
- **Agent ROI:** Can market "Free inspection with lease" or keep as margin

---

### B2B Wholesale Pricing

**Per-Report Pricing:**

| Commitment | Price per Report | DTC Equivalent | Discount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20–49 inspections/quarter | AED 300 | AED 450 avg | 33% |
| 50–99 inspections/quarter | AED 250 | AED 450 avg | 44% |
| 100+ inspections/quarter | AED 200 | AED 450 avg | 56% |

**Volume Kickback:**

| Annual Inspections | Kickback Rate | Annual Bonus (at AED 200/report) |
|---|---|---|
| 100–199 | 3% | AED 600–1,194 |
| 200–499 | 5% | AED 2,000–4,990 |
| 500+ | 8% | AED 8,000+ |

**Example (100 inspections/quarter):**
- AED 250 × 100 × 4 = AED 100,000/year baseline
- Kickback at 5%: AED 5,000/year
- Effective unit economics: AED 237.50 per report for Tenify

## 5. Go-to-Market Execution

### Phase 1: Pilot (Months 1–2)

**Goals:**
- Validate B2B demand and willingness to partner
- Refine value prop and pricing
- Prove co-branding and report integration work technically

**Activities:**
1. **Identify 3–5 target agencies** in your launch market (e.g., Dubai)
   - Mix of small (1–3 agents) and mid-size (5–10 agents)
   - Active in residential lettings, not just sales
   
2. **Outreach & pitch** referral model
   - 15% commission on DTC price
   - No volume commitment
   - Quick onboarding (1 page partner agreement)
   
3. **Partner enablement**
   - Email templates agents can send to customers
   - One-pager explaining "Why Tenify with [Agency Name]"
   - Branded digital assets (social media, WhatsApp graphics)
   
4. **Technical integration** (low-lift)
   - Custom referral link/code in booking flow (tracks source)
   - Co-branded report template (agency logo in header/footer)
   - Email notification to agent when booking originates from their referral code

---

### Phase 2: Optimize & Scale (Months 3–6)

**Goals:**
- Onboard 10–15 partners across referral + wholesale mix
- Refine wholesale terms based on pilot feedback
- Build repeatable partner sales + onboarding playbook

**Activities:**
1. **Wholesale upgrade path**
   - Identify referral partners generating 10+ referrals/month
   - Propose upgrade to wholesale with volume targets
   
2. **Co-branding & legal integration**
   - Finalize co-branding guidelines (logo placement, language)
   - Develop sample tenancy addendum language
   - Train partners on contractual positioning
   
3. **Partner success program**
   - Dedicate partner manager (part-time initially)
   - Quarterly reviews, performance dashboards
   - Marketing support (co-branded case studies, testimonials)
   
4. **Expand target geographies**
   - If Dubai pilot succeeds, expand to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah
   - Adapt messaging to local market (e.g., Expat-heavy vs. Local)

---

### Phase 3: Strategic Partnerships (Months 6+)

**Goals:**
- Target 2–3 enterprise/group partners (holding companies, large brokerages)
- Build platform for at-scale partnerships
- Establish Tenify as the **inspection standard** for UAE lettings market

**Activities:**
1. **Enterprise partnerships**
   - Approach large brokerage groups with 50+ agents
   - Custom pricing, white-label option, API integration
   
2. **Marketing co-op**
   - Explore co-marketing budgets (partner pays into shared digital campaigns)
   - Joint case studies, webinars, industry events
   
3. **Ecosystem integrations**
   - Integration with partner CRM/property management systems (e.g., Wasl, eQar)
   - Direct booking from agent dashboard (bypass public Tenify booking flow)

## 6. Legal & Operational Considerations

### Partner Agreement Essentials

**Referral Agreement (1-page template):**
- Partner definition and scope
- Commission rate and payment terms (net 30)
- Termination clause (30-day notice)
- IP/branding guidelines
- Non-compete (optional; light-touch for low-volume referrals)

**Wholesale Agreement (3–5 pages):**
- Volume commitments and pricing tiers
- Term (e.g., 12 months with auto-renew)
- Co-branding guidelines and approval process
- Liability & indemnification (agent liability for booking accuracy, Tenify for report quality)
- Termination and wind-down (reconciliation, final invoice)
- Data sharing & privacy (partner customer data handling)

---

### Insurance & Liability

- Inspect each partner's E&O (Errors & Omissions) / Professional Indemnity insurance
- Ensure Tenify is named as additional insured if available
- Confirm Tenify's own E&O covers reports delivered via third-party distribution

---

### Payment & Commission Tracking

**System Requirements:**
1. Dashboard to track referrals by source (partner code)
2. Monthly commission calculation and reporting
3. Stripe or bank transfer for payouts (net 30)
4. Partner self-service portal to view KPIs, commissions, reports

## 9. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Partners poach Tenify agents/customers | Medium | High | Clear T&Cs; non-poaching clause; brand the service strongly |
| Commission disputes or payment delays | Low | Medium | Clear agreement; automated tracking; monthly reporting |
| Quality dilution (agents pressure inspectors) | Medium | Medium | Partner agreements specify Tenify controls inspection quality; regular audits |
| Low partner adoption | Medium | High | Strong pilot phase; flexible pricing; dedicated partner success |
| Brand dilution (poor co-branding execution) | Low | Medium | Strict co-branding guidelines; approval process before launch |

## Appendix A: Market Context (UAE)

- **Estate agent density:** High competition; minimal differentiation
- **Expat population:** 80%+ of Dubai; high deposit dispute risk
- **Regulatory environment:** RERA, DTCM focus on consumer protection (tailwind for Tenify)
- **Tech adoption:** High; WhatsApp-first communication prevalent
- **Payment methods:** Stripe, bank transfer, WhatsApp Pay all common

---

## Appendix B: Competitive Positioning

### vs. Traditional Snagging Companies
- **Snagging:** New-build focus; structural defects; handover documentation
- **Tenify B2B:** Move-in baseline; pre-existing damage documentation; deposit protection

### vs. Direct Competitor Inspection Services
- **Tenify advantage:** B2B partnership model allows agents to monetise; co-branding creates institutional legitimacy
- **Tenify risk:** Competitors may launch same partnerships simultaneously; speed to market is key
