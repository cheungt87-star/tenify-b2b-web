# Tenify App — Alternative Color Palette Options

**Date:** August 16, 2026  
**Status:** Design Review — Color Alternatives  
**Current:** Cyber Teal (#03313A) + Ice Glass (#DFF7FF)

---

## Context

Current implementation uses Cyber Teal (#03313A) as primary dark color with Ice Glass (#DFF7FF) as accent. Reviewing app in browser shows modern tech aesthetic but may feel too cool-toned or harsh. Exploring alternative color palettes that maintain contrast and visual hierarchy while offering different brand personalities.

---

## OPTION 1: CYBER TEAL (CURRENT)
**Personality:** Tech-forward, modern, cool

### Palette
```
Primary Dark:  #03313A (Cyber Teal)
Primary Light: #DFF7FF (Ice Glass)
Hover/Medium:  #0D4A5C (Teal 600)
Background:    #F0F8FB (Teal 50)
```

### Pros
- ✅ High contrast (11.5:1 on white)
- ✅ Modern, tech brand feel
- ✅ Strong visual hierarchy
- ✅ Excellent accessibility (WCAG AA+)
- ✅ Cool, professional aesthetic

### Cons
- ⚠️ May feel cold/sterile on light backgrounds
- ⚠️ Stark contrast might be jarring on mobile
- ⚠️ Less warm/inviting than original jade
- ⚠️ Very different from brand history (Jade)

---

## OPTION 2: DEEP TEAL + CREAM
**Personality:** Balanced, premium, warm-cool hybrid

### Palette
```
Primary Dark:  #1B4D4A (Deep Teal - warmer)
Primary Light: #F5FFFE (Soft Cream - warmer white)
Hover/Medium:  #2A7B7A (Teal Medium)
Background:    #EEF7F6 (Teal Cream 50)
```

### Color Swatches
- Deep Teal (#1B4D4A): Slightly warmer, less stark
- Soft Cream (#F5FFFE): Warmer than pure white
- Teal Medium (#2A7B7A): Better hover contrast
- Teal Cream 50 (#EEF7F6): Warmer background tint

### Pros
- ✅ Warmer teal (less cold than Cyber Teal)
- ✅ Cream accent instead of bright white (softer)
- ✅ Premium, balanced feel
- ✅ Still high contrast (10.2:1 on cream)
- ✅ Better for longer reading sessions
- ✅ Maintains tech aesthetic with warmth

### Cons
- ⚠️ Slightly lower contrast than Cyber Teal
- ⚠️ Less dramatic brand shift from Jade
- ⚠️ Still different from original palette

### Contrast Check
- Deep Teal (#1B4D4A) on Soft Cream (#F5FFFE): ~10.2:1 ✅
- Deep Teal on white: ~11.8:1 ✅
- Teal Medium (#2A7B7A) on cream: ~8.1:1 ✅

## Visual Personality Comparison

### Warmest to Coolest
1. **Warmest:** Slate Blue + Gold (professional warmth)
2. Forest Green + Sage (natural warmth)
3. Deep Teal + Cream (balanced)
4. Ocean Blue + Seafoam (neutral)
5. Indigo + Lavender (cool elegance)
6. Cyber Teal (cool, tech)
7. **Coolest:** Charcoal + Cyan (bold cool)

### Highest to Lowest Contrast
1. **Highest:** Charcoal + Cyan (15:1+)
2. Cyber Teal (11.5:1)
3. Slate Blue + Gold (11.1:1)
4. Indigo + Lavender (10.8:1)
5. Forest Green + Sage (10.5:1)
6. Deep Teal + Cream (10.2:1)
7. **Lowest:** Ocean Blue + Seafoam (9.8:1)

### Tech Feel (1-5 Stars)
- **Most tech:** Cyber Teal (5⭐) & Charcoal + Cyan (5⭐)
- **Balanced:** Deep Teal + Cream, Ocean Blue + Seafoam, Indigo + Lavender (4⭐)
- **Less tech:** Slate Blue + Gold, Forest Green + Sage (3⭐)
